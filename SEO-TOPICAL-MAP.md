Updated 27 Sep 2026: sectors now match the five portfolio groups; see src/content/sectors.ts.

The complete cluster-level topical map. Entity model, borders and methodology: [`docs/seo/topical-map.md`](docs/seo/topical-map.md). Volumes: [`docs/seo/keyword-research.md`](docs/seo/keyword-research.md) (NP = Nepal, UK = United Kingdom, Google Ads monthly).

**Central entity:** Ample Associates · **Source context:** a Nepal-focused investment and project-development organisation, with a UK/Nepal diaspora audience · **Central search intent:** understand, evaluate and take part in projects in Nepal.

**Sectors (in the client's order):** Education Consultancy `/sectors/education-consultancy/` · College `/sectors/college/` · Energy Sector `/sectors/energy/` · Property Development `/sectors/property-development/` · Financial Channel `/sectors/financial-channel/`. The old URLs `/sectors/property/`, `/sectors/hospitality/`, `/sectors/renewable-energy/`, `/sectors/education/` and `/sectors/strategic-ventures/` 301-redirect to these (see `config/http-rules.mjs`).

**Status key:** `Live` = published at launch · `Planned` = brief ready, not written · `Future` = needs data or a project first
**Priority:** P1 launch · P2 first 3 months · P3 3-9 months

## Pages deliberately **not** created

| Query / idea                                          | Reason                                                                                                                                         |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "land for sale in pokhara" (140 / 50)                 | Listing intent; Ample does not sell plots. A page would mislead users.                                                                         |
| City-by-city "invest in X" pages                      | No unique information per city; mass/thin page risk (YMYL).                                                                                    |
| "hydropower companies in nepal" / NEPSE / IPO pages   | Stock-trading intent, outside the topical border.                                                                                              |
| "nrn nepal" (NRNA organisation)                       | Navigational to another entity.                                                                                                                |
| "begnas lake" (14,800 NP)                             | Travel intent (border). Used only as context inside the Lekhnath guide.                                                                        |
| Separate "investment opportunities in [sector]" pages | Would cannibalise sector pages; filters on `/investments/` serve this.                                                                         |
| Separate hospitality sector page                      | Hospitality is not a portfolio group. The Lakeside hotel is covered on `/sectors/property-development/` until a documented opportunity exists. |
| Separate Back2Nepal page outside `/sectors/`          | `/sectors/financial-channel/` is the entity page; the `#back2nepal` sections on `/` and `/investors/` summarise it.                            |
