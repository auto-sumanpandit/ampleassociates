## Method and limits

- **Search volume** = Google Ads average monthly searches over the last 12 months, per country. Google Ads rounds small volumes into buckets (10, 20, 30…), so small numbers are approximate.
- **Keyword difficulty** comes only from DataForSEO Labs, which **does not support Nepal** (location code 2524 is rejected). Nepal KD is therefore `DATA UNAVAILABLE`. UK KD is shown where Labs returned it.
- **US and other international** volumes were not pulled in this pass → `DATA UNAVAILABLE`. Recommended next pull: US (2840), Australia (2036), UAE (2784), Qatar (2634), all large Nepali diaspora markets.
- **Traffic potential / parent topic** (Ahrefs-style metrics): `DATA UNAVAILABLE`. Ahrefs was not connected in this session.
- CPC in Nepal is near zero for almost every term (highest: USD 0.69 for "housing in pokhara"). Paid competition is low, and so is commercial search maturity.
- No volumes were estimated or invented. Empty Google Ads results are shown as `DATA UNAVAILABLE`.

## Headline findings

1. **The "investment" head terms are small.** "invest in nepal" / "investment in nepal" get 590 searches a month in Nepal and 20 in the UK. "nepal investment opportunities", "property investment nepal", "hydropower investment nepal" and "hotel investment nepal" returned **no measurable volume** in Nepal.
2. **Demand is informational and local.** The largest relevant clusters are "hydropower in nepal" (880), "real estate in nepal" (720), "foreign investment in nepal" and "fdi in nepal" (480 each), and the place names "lekhnath" (3,600) and "pokhara lekhnath" (1,300).
3. **The UK diaspora searches for homes, not "investments".** In the UK, "house for sale in nepal" (140), "house for sale in pokhara" (70) and "land for sale in pokhara" (50) all outrank every "invest in Nepal" phrasing (20 or fewer).
4. **No SERP has credible developer-led content.** "invest in nepal" is owned by government portals and embassies. "house for sale in pokhara" is owned by listing portals and social-media agents. "nrn investment in nepal" goes to law firms and NEPSE share pages, and "hydropower in nepal" to trade bodies and academic PDFs. A transparent, project-led, sourced explainer is a gap Ample can fill.
5. **What this means for the site:** don't chase "investment" keywords with thin pages. Win on three things:
   - a genuinely useful pillar on how investing in Nepal works
   - the Ample Cozy Homes page plus the Pokhara Lekhnath buyer guide, for diaspora home-buyer intent
   - sourced sector explainers
6. **Diaspora investment intent is small but high-value.** "nrn investment in nepal" gets 10 a month in each country, "nepal investment company" 20 in Nepal, and "invest in nepal from uk" has no measurable UK volume. Ample now has a real entity for this intent, NRN Back 2 Nepal Investment Company (`/sectors/financial-channel/`). Serve it there and in the overseas and NRN guides; don't build extra keyword pages for it.
