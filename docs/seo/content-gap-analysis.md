## 4. Rules for adding content

1. A new page needs a distinct intent **and** enough unique, verifiable information. Otherwise add a section to an existing page.
2. Every statistic needs publisher, reference year and last-verified date.
3. YMYL legal content is reviewed by a qualified lawyer before publishing.
4. No fixed word counts: answer the question completely, answer first.
5. No content for businesses that are no longer in the portfolio. Education Consultancy and College pages stay short and branded; study-abroad and admissions content belongs to ampleedu.com and SAMS College London.
