The Historical Foundation

From Orbit International to a dual-nation bridge.

In 2004, the journey began as an office boy at Orbit International Education in Pokhara. Carrying files, opening doors, and observing the hopes of young students seeking foreign education instilled an early reverence for cross-border ambition.

[TO BE CONFIRMED]

By 2009 [TO BE CONFIRMED detail], persistent dedication paved the pathway to deeper operational responsibility, navigating compliance, student counselling, and institutional partnerships.

In 2010, the foundation crossed continents with a relocation to the United Kingdom, mastering financial structures, regulatory standards, and capital governance in London while keeping institutional ties grounded in Nepal.

"Every company we build serves Nepali families, at home and abroad."

Over the following decade, the work expanded to guide more than 4,500 students into reputable universities worldwide. That single education outpost evolved systematically into Ample Associates, a dual-nation enterprise uniting five core sectors: education advisory, accredited colleges, renewable run-of-the-river hydropower, eco-conscious hospitality property development, and institutional remittance channels.
