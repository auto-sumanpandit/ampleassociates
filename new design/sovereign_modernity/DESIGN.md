## Brand & Style

This design system expresses quiet confidence, institutional gravitas, and progressive bridge-building between the financial capital of London and the sovereign potential of Nepal. It balances London's razor-sharp financial rigor with the timeless serenity and scale of the Himalayas. 

The aesthetic is tailored corporate editorial: expansive whitespace, exacting proportions, structural hairline dividers, and high-impact structural typography. The visual language purposefully avoids generic tech-startup playfulness in favor of poised, sovereign authority. Rich deep navy foundations anchor electric royal blue accents, yielding a polished, high-conviction medium for international cross-border investment, sovereign wealth, and bespoke institutional advisory.

## Colors

The palette establishes a disciplined transatlantic dialogue between formal corporate architecture and vibrant technological foresight:

- **Primary & Secondary Tones:** Royal Blue (`#3D03FA`) serves as the active catalyst for focal elements and key actions, supported by Indigo (`#240294`) for active states and high-contrast anchors. Sky Blue (`#2287FD`) brings luminous clarity to informational markers, charts, and secondary accents.
- **Deep Anchors & Neutrals:** Deep Navy (`#070D26`) and Navy (`#0B1437`) establish the institutional bedrock, used for key editorial layouts, high-impact cards, and primary typography. Slate (`#475569`) delivers authoritative secondary reading contrast, supported by Slate Light (`#94A3B8`) for muted meta-labels and structural metrics.
- **Atmospheric Surfaces:** Lavender Tint (`#F4F1FF`) and Lavender Soft (`#E9E3FF`) form low-contrast surface foundations that keep long-form reading calm and distinctly bespoke compared to standard monochrome grays. Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) remains the crisp baseline.
- **Borders & Rules:** Structure is preserved via ultra-refined hairlines: Light Slate Border (`#E2E8F0`) for daylit surfaces and Translucent White (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`) across deep navy layers.
- **Status & Accent:** Amber Dark (`#B45309`) over Amber Pale (`#FEF3C7`) denotes curated strategic highlights, discreet transactional notices, and verified asset indicators.

## Typography

The type system relies on Poppins across all levels, relying on stark typographic weight juxtaposition to convey an editorial, high-finance tone:

- **Display & Large Headlines:** Ultra-light weights (`200` and `300`) pair with tight tracking to evoke the quiet grandeur of Himalayan peaks alongside Mayfair executive restraint.
- **Editorial Sub-Headlines & Titles:** Semi-bold (`600`) grounds section intros and key stats with unwavering structural stability.
- **Body Text:** Standard weight (`400`) at comfortable line heights provides effortless reading for technical disclosures, portfolio theses, and cross-border regulatory analyses.
- **Labels & Micro-data:** Uppercase, widely-tracked `label-sm` (`0.08em` tracking at `600` weight) establishes institutional order across data tables, category pills, and transactional states.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed on an authoritative 12-column grid designed for vast, deliberate negative space:

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column fluid grid locked to a max width of 1440px, featuring generous outer canvas margins of `4rem` (`64px`) and `2rem` (`32px`) gutters. Editorial spreads often offset headline copy across 5 columns while leaving adjacent columns vacant to evoke spacious luxury.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with `2.5rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Multi-column editorial cards condense into 2-column modules.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Stacks vertically with strict adherence to the `space-md` (`16px`) and `space-xl` (`48px`) vertical rhythm.
- **Hairline Framing:** Grid modules and canvas sections are visually bounded by 1px hairlines (`#E2E8F0` on light, `rgba(255,255,255,0.12)` on dark), creating clean, architectural segmentation reminiscent of classical financial prospectuses.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy employs a multi-tiered system combining subtle ambient elevation and architectural translucency:

- **Flat Architectural Baseline:** Standard panels lie flush, anchored by 1px hairlines without elevation.
- **Layered Ambient Shadows:** Elevated surfaces (e.g., hovering cards, bespoke selectors, modal dossiers) cast dual-stop, diffused shadows tinted with deep navy:
  - *Standard Card Lift:* `0 10px 30px -10px rgba(11, 20, 55, 0.08), 0 20px 40px -15px rgba(61, 3, 250, 0.04)`
  - *Floating Focus Lift:* `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(7, 13, 38, 0.16), 0 32px 64px -20px rgba(34, 135, 253, 0.08)`
- **Atmospheric Navy Overlays:** Deep institutional cards (`#070D26`) employ backdrops paired with crisp `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` borders to achieve a floating jewel effect against lighter page sections.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates deliberate, structural curvature to marry British institutional refinement with fluid, mountain-inspired forms:

- **Pill Geometry (`9999px`):** Used universally for all interactive CTA buttons, filtering pills, badge tags, and floating search elements.
- **Distinctive Container Curvature:** Primary editorial feature cards, institutional overview containers, and showcase blocks use an intentional **28px** radius, graduating to **36px** on hero display banners and hero containers.
- **Sub-elements & Nested Containers:** Form inputs, dropdown menus, and internal card items feature a calibrated **12px** to **16px** radius, preserving clear visual nested hierarchy inside larger 28px/36px boundaries.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Full pill (`roundedness: 3`), `#3D03FA` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `Poppins 600` uppercase tracking. Hover shifts gracefully to `#240294` with a soft royal blue glow.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Full pill, transparent fill, 1px `#0B1437` or `#E2E8F0` hairline border, `#0B1437` text. Hover shifts to `#F4F1FF`.
- **Inverted Dark Surface:** Full pill, `#FFFFFF` text with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` border. Hover introduces `#3D03FA` fill.

### Cards
- **Institutional Metric Card:** 28px border radius, pure `#FFFFFF` fill, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, generous internal padding (`space-xl`). Displays stat values in Poppins Light (`200`) accented by royal blue markers.
- **Executive Feature Block:** 36px border radius, deep `#070D26` fill, 1px `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` border. Body copy in `#94A3B8` paired with Sky Blue (`#2287FD`) micro-labels.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status & Category Pills:** Full pill profile, `space-xs` vertical and `space-md` horizontal padding. Typography matches `label-sm`.
- **Amber Verification Badge:** Light amber background (`#FEF3C7`), rich amber label (`#B45309`), signaling verified regulatory or financial validation.

### Inputs & Selectors
- **Form Fields:** Height 52px, 14px border radius, `#FFFFFF` background with 1px `#E2E8F0` hairline border. Focused state transitions border to `#3D03FA` with an ambient `0 0 0 3px #F4F1FF` focus ring.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** High-precision 20px custom controls. Active state features a rich `#3D03FA` fill with crisp white iconography.

### Editorial & Financial Elements
- **Hairline Dividers:** 1px rules spanning columns, paired with `space-xl` margins to delineate balance sheets, leadership commentary, and bilateral venture milestones.
- **Dual-Market Metric Gauges:** Paired KPI data displays balancing London time/indices (GBP/LSE) against Nepal infrastructure and fiscal tickers (NPR/NEPSE) with ultra-light display numbers.
