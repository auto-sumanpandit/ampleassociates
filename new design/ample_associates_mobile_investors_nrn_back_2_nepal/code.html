FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear answers on diaspora investment.

Statutory transparency and governance parameters for international Nepali investors.

Non-Resident Nepalis (NRNs) holding valid diaspora documentation, foreign passports of Nepali origin, or dual-jurisdiction residency credentials. The vehicle is engineered specifically in accordance with Nepal foreign investment legislation and bilateral remittances.
The base institutional entry tier is NPR 5,00,000 (five lakh Nepali Rupees), denominated and registered in Nepal. Diaspora subscriptions from the UK, Europe, Middle East, and APAC can be remitted in GBP, USD, or EUR through designated statutory custodial channels with official central bank conversion certification.
Every subscription is formally documented with official share certificates issued under the Office of the Company Registrar (OCR) in Kathmandu. Investors receive complete legal titles, audited bi-annual governance reports, and cross-border custodial confirmations accessible through our investor relations portal.
Allocations operate on medium-to-long term horizons (typically 3 to 7 years) aligned with foundational infrastructure and real estate delivery. Repatriation routes are structured under Nepal Rastra Bank guidelines for foreign exchange repatriation for registered foreign capital infusions.
Senior leadership oversees direct bilateral board administration split between London advisory headquarters and operational ground units in Kathmandu and Pokhara. We execute transparent dual-audits, third-party governance reviews, and asset-backed custody.
