The Historical Foundation

From Orbit International to a dual-nation bridge.

Ample Associates began in 2004 with a single desk in Pokhara. Serving initially as an office attendant at Orbit International Education, our founder gained first-hand insight into the aspirations of Nepali families seeking global horizons.
