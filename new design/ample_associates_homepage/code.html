Institutional Heritage

From a Pokhara office to a UK and Nepal company.

Pioneering cross-border capital, resilient infrastructure, and educational mobility between Kathmandu, Pokhara, and the City of London.

"It started with an office job in Pokhara."

Beginning as an office boy at Orbit International Education in Pokhara, the ambition was never merely local. The journey required moving to the United Kingdom, mastering financial systems, and systematically forging connections with sovereign and diaspora capital.

Today, Ample Associates represents a multi-tiered venture ecosystem. From large-scale clean hydropower generation to modern European campuses and residential developments, every project remains committed to long-term bilateral growth.
